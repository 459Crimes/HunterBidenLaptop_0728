## What “unknown origin” means

**Unknown origin** means: after exact-hash comparison to the named catalogs, the blob is **not accounted for**. It does not mean “proved hacked,” “proved planted,” or “proved from another person’s computer.” Those are **hypotheses** for the 94,635-hash remainder. The author’s 28 July 2026 FBI referral treats hacking as a **potential**; this encyclopedia records that referral as project identity and keeps the hash remainder unlabeled.
