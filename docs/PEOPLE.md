## Conan James Hayes

Central human subject of this encyclopedia. Public-record roles that attach to 0728:

- **Alleged upstream** of the Extra Found Files MEGA share (Ziegler’s attribution; Hayes’s later method claim).
- **Direct provider** of the June 2022 APFS image to this author (not 0728).
- **Speaker A** on the 17 April 2024 recorded call **R-00014**, describing carving Extra Files from unallocated space.
- **Named subject** of FBI Form FD-597 dated **13 September 2022** (black iPhone).
- **Mesa County (May 2021)** actor: used Gerald Wood’s badge during the trusted build; made a forensic image of the elections server (Colorado trial / appellate recitation).

Longer treatment: [Conan Hayes](CONAN_HAYES.md). Call: [R-00014](R00014_CALL.md). Phone event: [Phone seizure](PHONE_SEIZURE.md). Election case: [Tina Peters](TINA_PETERS.md).

Press accounts describe Hayes as a **retired professional surfer** and **co-founder of RVCA**. Those biographical labels are **contemporaneous journalism**, not 0728 filesystem facts.
