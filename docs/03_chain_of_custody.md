A chain of custody is a sequence of **identified handlers and media**. For 0728 the chain is **short at the project end and broken at the recovery end**.

**Attributed, not session-proved:** Hayes as the person who ran the recovery.

Those events explain **other corpora**. Putting them on the 0728 chain as if they were parents of Extra Found Files is a category error unless a hash or transfer record says so.
