# Tina Peters / Mesa County (Hayes)

> **Hatnote.** Court-recited election-system event attached to Hayes. It is **not** Extra Found Files. It is in this encyclopedia because it is Hayes’s best-documented forensic imaging act and the leading competing context for the September 2022 iPhone event.

## Undisputed core (trial / appellate recitation)

| Date | Event |
|---|---|
| May 2021 | Mesa County Dominion “trusted build” |
| May 2021 | Clerk **Tina Peters** obtained a badge for consultant **Gerald Wood** |
| May 2021 | Badge given to deputy **Belinda Knisley**; used by **Conan Hayes** |
| May 2021 | Hayes, introduced as Wood, made a forensic image of the elections server |
| Aug 2021 | Passwords/images leaked; investigation; Peters later convicted at trial (Aug 2024) |

Wood testified he did not authorize Hayes to use the badge and was not at the trusted build.

Appellate opinion: *People v. Peters*, Colo. App. No. **24CA1951** (summary published April 2026), https://www.coloradojudicial.gov/system/files/opinions-2026-04/24CA1951-PD.pdf — trusted-build facts ¶¶11–14; excluded informant-belief evidence ¶89.

## Informant defense (excluded)

Defense attorney Daniel Hartman argued Peters believed Hayes was a governmental informant and concealed his identity. Judge Matthew Barrett kept it from the jury. DA Dan Rubinstein told the court the **FBI confirmed Hayes never was an informant**. CPR, Colorado Sun, Colorado Newsline, and Seattle Times reported the fight in August 2024.

R-00014’s Homeland/Backpage self-description does **not** fill that gap.
