## Phone seizure (Hayes’s telling)

Hayes said he spoke with Trump days before the Mar-a-Lago raid; a week-ish later he was pulled over in **California**; agents in a van had a **search warrant**; he watched an agent **power the phone off and on**, disabling biometrics; the warrant as he read it allowed fingerprint/face, not passcode; he considered the phone “useless” to them; he attributed the stop “100%” to the Trump call; one agent claimed Austin but was “really out of DC.”

Independent paper in this repo: FD-597 **13 Sep 2022** (months after Mar-a-Lago, ~13 months after Mesa County). Hayes’s “week later” compression does not match that date. See [Phone seizure](PHONE_SEIZURE.md).

**00:25:07–00:25:46.** Hayes: worked for Homeland before the election; helped FBI on the Backpage case; “I come in and out… I’m not associated or affiliated.” Contract cancelled when (in his telling) border budget was cut.
