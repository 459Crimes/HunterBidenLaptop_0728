## Clip 1 — Trump / Mar-a-Lago / phone stop

Audio: [`R-00014_trump_maralago.mp3`](exhibits/audio/R-00014_trump_maralago.mp3) (SHA-256 `3578c114fb6e923171ab54f1a2751f4dd7d967c17b74e36ff800a2e604d693fd`)

Hayes said he spoke with Trump **days before** the Mar-a-Lago search; Trump wanted help finding someone leaking around him; later he was pulled over in **California**, placed in a van with a **search warrant**; an agent **powered the phone off and on**, which (Hayes said) disabled biometrics; the warrant as he read it allowed fingerprint/face, not passcode; agents kept the phone; he attributed the stop “**100%**” to the Trump call; one agent claimed Austin but was “really out of DC.”

**Limitation.** This is Hayes’s telling. The photographed FD-597 is dated **13 September 2022**, not “a week” after 8 August. [Phone seizure](PHONE_SEIZURE.md).
