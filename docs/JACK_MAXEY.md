# Jack Maxey

> **Hatnote.** Public “deleted files / 400 GB” claimant. Not a hash-matched 0728 custodian in this repository.

**Jack Maxey** is a commentator who, in 2022, publicized a whistleblower story and large “newly recovered / deleted” Hunter Biden file-size figures. On Patrick Byrne’s **8 April 2022** Locals stream, the chat asked whether Maxey was **exaggerating the size of the deleted data** (“400GB’s?”). Byrne answered with **400,000 files**, said Maxey’s **byte** estimate sounded high but not implausible, and claimed he (Byrne) had the files. [Patrick Byrne](PATRICK_BYRNE.md).

The author, in that same chat, wrote that **Maxey is starving for attention** and does not know how to use a laptop — a participant jab, not a forensic finding.

## What the numbers probably mixed

Parent-project notes treat Maxey’s **~450 GB** class of remarks as a likely **conflation** of:

| Object | Approx. size |
|---|---|
| 500 GB-class **SanDisk** (bootable APFS family) | drive capacity |
| Extra Found Files **logical** mass | ~313 GiB (canonical) / ~170 GB on the 5 Aug 2021 Properties screenshot |
| “400,000 files” | close to Extra Found Files **path count** |

Until Maxey publishes hashes, his cache is a **lead**, not a fourth primary source.

## Daily Mail / Switzerland (context)

Maxey later described fleeing with material and handing files to members of Congress ([Daily Mail](https://www.dailymail.co.uk/news/article-10689445/Whistleblower-handed-Hunter-Bidens-laptop-congressmen-fled-Switzerland.html) — often paywalled). That is **Maxey’s publicity**, not a 0728 path row.
